# Overview Hydraulic Parameters

## 1. No freezing 
## 1.1. Water retention curve $\frac{\partial \theta_l}{\partial h_c}$
van Genuchten:  

$$
\theta_l=\theta_r+(\theta_s-\theta_r)\left(1+(\alpha h_c)^n\right)^{\frac{1-n}{n}}
$$     
#### 1.2. relative hydraulic conductivity $k_{rel}$
note that    
  
$$
S_e=\frac{\theta_l-\theta_r}{\theta_s-\theta_r}
$$
  
van Genuchten:   
  
$$
k_{rel}(S_e)=\sqrt{Se}\left(1-\left(1-S_e^\frac{n}{n-1}\right)^\frac{n-1}{n}\right)^2
$$   
## 2. With freezing
## 2.1.  Pflotran - Ice  
Assumes $\theta_i$ can be treated as $\theta_a$ in van Genuchten parametrisation

## 2.2 PermaFOAM [1]
  
applies impedance factor  
   
$$
k_f = k_{rel}max(10^{-\Omega\theta_i};k_{fmin})
$$

## 2.4 Soil Freezing Curve (SFC)
### 2.4.1 Not based on SWC

If $T$ greater than $T_f$    
   
$$
\theta_l = \text{solution to Richards equation}
$$
  
else   
  
$$
\theta_l=\theta_r+(\theta_s-\theta_r)\exp\left(-\left(\frac{T-T_f}{\omega}\right)^2\right) \tag{PC}
$$
  
$$
\theta_i=\theta_{rich}-\theta_l
$$
  
This is implemented in PermaFOAM [1].
### 2.4.2 Based on SWC
The phase-change equation $(PC)$ is an empirical parametrisation, which is not based on the Clausius-Claperyon equation. The water content could be relate to temperature with the CC equation,  which relates the temperature to pressure under the assumption of thermal equilibrium. When neglecting the deformation of soil due to freezing/thawing the equation is given by:
  
$$
\frac{\partial P_w}{\partial T}\approx \frac{H_f\rho_l}{T}
$$
Integrating with respect to $T$ results in 
$$
P_w = H_f\rho_l \ln(T)\Leftrightarrow h_w=\frac{H_f}{g}\ln(T)
$$
